**66 checks errored + 6 skipped = 60% of the check registry couldn't complete.** This is a major reliability problem.

### C1 — Browser collector timeouts (6 checks skipped)

All 6 checks that need Playwright browser to load the page failed with:
```
browser:context, browser:total-timeout
```

Affected checks:
- `failed-network-requests`
- `js-exceptions-detected`
- `mixed-content-on-https-page`
- `js-exception-regression`
- `hydration-errors-detected`
- `critical-page-blank-or-error`

**Suspected root cause:** Playwright can't complete `page.goto()` on the Vercel-hosted testbed within the timeout. Could be:
- Vercel's cold-start delay

**Impact for BugBuzzer:** Every scan against a Vercel/serverless site will have these 6 checks silently skipped. Users won't know their JS runtime error surface is unchecked.
